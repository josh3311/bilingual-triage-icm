Behind every ticket, seven steps

ScriptIntakeRejects incomplete tickets. Tags English or French.
ClaudeClassifyPicks a category and a priority.
ScriptRouteOne category, one team. Never guesses.
ScriptLookupFinds the customer, order, charges and policy in the records.
ClaudeReplyDrafts 3 to 5 sentences from those facts, in the customer's language.
ScriptAuditChecks a fixed sample of 20 tickets, 8 ways, facts included.
A personApprovalApproves or returns. Nothing is ever sent.

Blue is Claude reading and writing. Green is a script counting and checking. Amber is a person deciding.

What changes when the work is split into checked stages

One long AI chat

  • Tickets can be dropped or counted wrong, and nobody notices.
  • A reply can promise a refund or a deadline nobody approved.
  • A reply can state an amount or a delivery status nobody looked up.
  • French tickets can get English replies.
  • When something is wrong, you re-read the whole chat to find where.

This workspace

  • A script counts tickets in and out of every stage.
  • A script looks up the customer's records before any reply is written.
  • A script checks every amount, date and status in a draft against those records.
  • A script blocks promise phrases and team names in drafts.
  • A script checks each draft is in the ticket's language.
  • A failed audit names the one stage to fix.

You can check all of this below in two minutes. Break a ticket on purpose, or make Claude invent a fact, and watch the audit catch it. Everything runs in your browser, uses no tokens, and uses 30 invented tickets.
