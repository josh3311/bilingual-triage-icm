What changes when the work is split into checked stages

One long AI chat

  • Tickets can be dropped or counted wrong, and nobody notices.
  • A reply can promise a refund or a deadline nobody approved.
  • French tickets can get English replies.
  • When something is wrong, you re-read the whole chat to find where.

This workspace

  • A script counts tickets in and out of every stage.
  • A script blocks promise phrases and team names in drafts.
  • A script checks each draft is in the ticket's language.
  • A failed audit names the one stage to fix.

You can check all of this below in two minutes. Break a ticket on purpose and watch the audit catch it. Everything runs in your browser, uses no tokens, and uses 30 invented tickets.
